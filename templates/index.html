HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about CampusConnect.

1. What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a digital platform designed to help students access campus services and information.

2. How can I register?

Visit the registration page and fill in the required details to create your account.

3. Can I submit complaints online?

Yes, registered students can submit campus-related complaints through the platform.

4. Can I track my complaint?

Yes, students can check the latest status of their submitted complaints.

5. Where can I find college notices?

You can visit the College Notices section to view available announcements.

6. Is registration required?

Yes, you need an account to access student-specific services.
